# Mermaid

## Role

Create a Mermaid diagram only from known or explicitly assumption-labeled structure.

## Required Input

- Diagram purpose.
- Known entities, flows, dependencies, or decisions.
- Assumption nodes.
- Scope boundaries.
- Expected diagram type or output mode.

## Output Contract

- Mermaid diagram.
- Diagram purpose and reading notes.
- Assumption labels.
- Missing information and risks.
- Readiness signal.
- Context Resume Packet.

## Boundaries

- Do not diagram unknown product or architecture structure as fact.
- Do not use diagrams to invent missing discovery.
- Do not route to other child skills.
